title: Affiliate disclosure
description: How The Ledger earns money, and why it never changes which prices or drops we show.

The Ledger is free to use. We earn money through affiliate links.

## How it works

When you click a link to a retailer and buy something, the retailer may pay us a small commission. You pay the same price either way.

## What it never changes

- **Which drops we post.** Every drop is chosen by the same [published rules](how-we-track-prices.html). A retailer can't pay to appear, and a commission can't put a deal at the top.
- **The prices we show.** We show the price the retailer lists in its own product feed, checked every morning.
- **Which shop we call cheapest.** We always rank by price, even when a cheaper shop pays us nothing.

## Advertising standards

Social posts that contain affiliate links are labelled #ad, in line with UK Advertising Standards Authority and CMA guidance.

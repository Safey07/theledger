The Ledger is a UK fragrance price tracker. We keep the data we handle to a minimum.

## What we collect

- **No accounts, no forms.** You can use the whole site without giving us any personal information.
- **No tracking cookies.** The Ledger does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The only thing we ever store on your device is a small setting that remembers if you turned analytics off (see below).
- **Anonymous visit statistics.** We use PostHog, hosted in the EU, in its cookieless mode to count page views and clicks through to retailers, and to spot pages that break. It does not use cookies or local storage, does not build a profile of you, and does not keep your IP address. We use these numbers only to improve the site. You can turn this off at any time (see below). See [PostHog's privacy policy](https://posthog.com/privacy).

<!-- COMPLIANCE CHECK: the sentence above says the IP address is not kept. Confirm "Discard client IP data" is switched ON in the PostHog project settings, or remove that claim. The site code does not enforce it. -->
- **Server logs.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Email.** If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

## Turn off analytics

You can object to our anonymous visit statistics at no cost. Use the button below and this browser will stop loading analytics on The Ledger. The choice is saved on your device in a single setting called ledger_analytics_optout, and applies to this browser only. Clearing your site data removes it, and you can switch analytics back on here at any time.

## When you click through to a retailer

Links to retailers may be affiliate links. When you click one, the retailer or its affiliate network may set cookies to record that you came from The Ledger, so we can earn a commission if you buy. Those cookies are controlled by the retailer or network, under their own privacy policies.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

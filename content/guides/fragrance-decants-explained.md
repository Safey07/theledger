title: Fragrance decants: a cheap way to try before you buy?
description: What fragrance decants are, why people buy them, and the risks to weigh before buying perfume that someone else has rebottled.

Decants are a popular way to try expensive fragrance without buying a full bottle. They can be useful, but they come with trade-offs worth understanding first.

## What a decant is

A decant is fragrance taken from a full bottle and transferred into a small atomiser, often between 2ml and 10ml, by a reseller. You're buying a portion of someone else's bottle, not a product the brand made.

## Why people buy them

- **To test before committing.** Wearing a scent for a few days tells you far more than a paper strip in a shop.
- **To try niche fragrance cheaply.** A small decant costs much less in total than a full niche bottle.
- **Variety.** Several small decants can suit people who like to rotate scents.

## The risks

**You can't check authenticity.** There's no sealed box or bottle. You're trusting the seller's word about what's inside.

**Handling and hygiene.** The fragrance has been opened, transferred and rebottled. How carefully that was done is up to the seller.

**Labelling.** Decants often lack the full ingredient and allergen information printed on the original packaging. This matters if you have sensitive skin.

**Price per ml is usually high.** Decants are convenient, not cheap. Per ml, they almost always cost more than the full bottle.

## Do the maths

Divide the decant price by its size to get a price per ml, then compare it with the full bottle's price per ml. If you're confident you like a scent, the full bottle is nearly always better value. See [EDT vs EDP vs parfum](edt-vs-edp-vs-parfum-value.html) for comparing value.

## Alternatives worth trying first

- **Testing in store.** Spray on skin, not paper, and wait a few hours.
- **Official samples** from the brand or retailer, often free with a purchase.
- **Discovery sets.** Many brands sell boxed sets of small vials of their range. They're sealed, labelled and sold by authorised retailers.
- **Travel sizes.** Small official bottles cost more per ml than a full bottle but less in total.

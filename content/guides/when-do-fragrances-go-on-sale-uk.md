title: When do designer fragrances go on sale in the UK?
description: The times of year UK retailers usually discount designer and niche fragrances, and how to time your purchase for the best price.

Fragrance prices in the UK follow a fairly predictable yearly rhythm. If you're not in a hurry, timing your purchase can save a meaningful amount.

## The big discount windows

**Black Friday and Cyber Monday (late November).** The single biggest discount period of the year for fragrance. Many department stores and beauty retailers run site-wide offers, and gift sets are often reduced too.

**Boxing Day and the January sales.** Leftover Christmas stock, especially gift sets, is cleared from Boxing Day into January. Good for sets, less reliable for single bottles.

**Summer sales (late June to July).** Mid-year clearance at department stores often includes fragrance, particularly older or seasonal releases.

**Big online shopping events.** Large marketplaces run major sale events in summer and autumn, and competing retailers often match them.

## The quieter windows

**Mother's Day and Father's Day.** Retailers promote fragrance heavily in the weeks before. You'll see more gift-with-purchase offers than straight price cuts.

**New release cycles.** When a brand launches a new version or "flanker", the older version is sometimes discounted to clear space.

## The worst time to buy

The weeks just before Christmas are often the most expensive, as demand peaks and discounts shrink. If you're buying a gift, buying earlier, around Black Friday, usually beats buying in mid-December.

## Don't rely on the calendar alone

Retailers also cut prices at random times to clear stock or match competitors. Those unplanned drops are often the best deals, and they are exactly what The Ledger checks for every morning.

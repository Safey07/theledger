title: Are fragrance gift sets good value? How to do the maths
description: A simple way to work out whether a fragrance gift set is cheaper than buying the bottle on its own, using price per ml.

Gift sets look generous: a bottle, a mini spray, maybe a body lotion, all in one box. Sometimes they're excellent value. Sometimes you're paying extra for things you'll never use. A few minutes of maths tells you which.

## Step 1: find the bottle on its own

Check what the same bottle sells for alone. It must be the **same size and the same concentration**. A set with a 90ml eau de toilette isn't comparable with a 100ml eau de parfum. See [EDT vs EDP vs parfum](edt-vs-edp-vs-parfum-value.html).

## Step 2: compare the prices

- **Set costs less than the bottle alone:** you're getting the extras for free, or better. Usually a good buy.
- **Set costs more:** the difference is what you're paying for the extras. Decide if they're worth it.

## Step 3: value the extras honestly

**Mini sprays of the same scent** have real value. Work it out at the main bottle's price per ml: divide the bottle price by its size, then multiply by the mini's size.

**Body lotion, shower gel and pouches** are worth something only if you'd buy them anyway. If not, count them as zero.

## A worked example (made-up numbers)

These figures are invented to show the method. They are not real prices.

- Bottle alone: 100ml for £80, so £0.80 per ml.
- Gift set: the same 100ml bottle plus a 10ml travel spray and a body lotion, for £85.
- The travel spray is 10ml × £0.80 = £8 of the same fragrance.
- So you pay £5 extra for £8 of fragrance, plus a lotion. That's good value, if you'll use the travel spray.

If the same set cost £95, you'd be paying £15 extra for £8 of fragrance and a lotion. Only worth it if you really want the lotion.

## Watch out for

- **Smaller bottles.** Some sets contain a smaller bottle than the standard sizes. Always check the ml.
- **Older stock.** Sets are seasonal. Leftover sets can sit in stock for a while.
- **"Worth" claims.** A box saying "worth £120" isn't a price anyone paid. Ignore it and do your own sums. See [how to tell if a deal is real](how-to-tell-if-a-fragrance-deal-is-real.html).

## When sets are cheapest

Sets arrive before Christmas, Mother's Day and Father's Day. Leftovers are often reduced after Christmas, from Boxing Day into January. Read [when fragrances go on sale](when-do-fragrances-go-on-sale-uk.html).
